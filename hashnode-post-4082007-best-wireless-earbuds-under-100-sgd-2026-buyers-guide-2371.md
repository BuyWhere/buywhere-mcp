## What to look for under $100 SGD

Three things matter more than brand at this price:

1. **Driver size and tuning** — 10mm dynamic drivers with a bass-elevated tuning now standard; look for app EQ if you want neutral sound.
2. **Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)** — Hybrid or adaptive ANC was a $200+ feature in 2023. In 2026 you can find it on the QCY HT07 ($45) and Soundcore Life P3i ($69).
3. **Battery life with ANC on** — Vendor specs usually quote ANC-off numbers. Realistic ANC-on battery is 60-70% of the headline figure.

Skip: spatial audio, head-tracking, premium codecs (LDAC is rare under $100). These are nice-to-haves that drive cost up.

## Common mistakes

1. **Buying the brand, not the spec.** The cheapest Samsung or Sony earbuds under $100 have weaker ANC and battery than the $50 QCY HT07. Brand premium is real at this tier.
2. **Trusting the headline battery.** "30h battery" usually means case + buds, ANC off. With ANC on, expect 6-9 hours.
3. **Skipping water resistance.** Singapore humidity + sudden rain = daily IPX4 minimum. Anything below is risky.
4. **Ignoring codec support.** If you have an Android phone, LDAC is a meaningful upgrade. iPhone users can ignore this — AAC is the ceiling.
