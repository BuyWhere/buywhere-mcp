## How to Choose the Right Gaming Laptop in 2026

- **Prioritize the GPU first.** The GPU drives gaming frame rates more than the CPU. RTX 5070 is the minimum we recommend for 1440p in 2026; RTX 4060 is fine only for 1080p or strict budget.
- **Match RAM to your workload.** 16GB is enough for most gamers. Choose 32GB if you stream (OBS + game + chat), edit video, run creative apps like Blender, or want more headroom for future games.
- **Check upgradeability.** Look for upgradeable RAM and at least one empty M.2 slot. Most 2026 gaming laptops still ship with 16GB soldered + 1 SODIMM slot; the Lenovo Legion Pro 7i and ASUS TUF A15 are the most upgradeable.
- **Display matters more than you think.** 240Hz IPS is the new standard for RTX 5070+; 165Hz is fine for RTX 4060. OLED (Zephyrus G16, Razer Blade 16, Omen Transcend 14) gives the best HDR and color but costs $200–$400 more.
- **Battery life is overrated on gaming laptops.** Expect 4–6 hours of light use; gaming on battery throttles hard. Plug in for serious play.
