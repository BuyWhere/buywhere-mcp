## Where the Price Difference Comes From

Singapore retailers price the LG C4 in three tiers:

1. **LG Official Store** — matches MSRP. You get full LG warranty and the best trade-in deals.

2. **E-commerce official stores** (Shopee/Lazada LG Official) — S$20-60 below MSRP. These run periodic flash sales that temporarily drop prices another 5-10%.

3. **Big-box electronics** (Courts, Harvey Norman, Best Denki, Gain City) — MSRP price but bundle freebies: soundbars, wall mounts, installation. The effective price drops when you value the bundle at S$100-300.
