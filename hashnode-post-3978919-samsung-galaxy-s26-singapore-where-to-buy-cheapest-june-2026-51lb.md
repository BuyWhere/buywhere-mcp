## Where the Price Discrepancy Comes From

Singapore mobile retailers price the Galaxy S26 series in three tiers:

1. **Official channels** — Samsung Online Store, Lazada SG official, Shopee SG official, Amazon SG. These match MSRP within ±S$20 and rarely run street discounts below 5%.

2. **Big-box electronics** — Courts, Harvey Norman, Best Denki, Challenger. They run periodic member-only sales (10–15% off) and bundle freebies. The headline cash price stays high but the effective price drops once you value the bundle.

3. **Marketplaces (third-party sellers)** — Lazada/Shopee with non-official sellers. **Avoid these** for new Galaxy S26 stock — they often sell grey-market imports, lack Singapore warranty, and the headline discount is fictional.
