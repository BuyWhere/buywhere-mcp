### What to look for to avoid scams

The Switch 2 is heavily counterfeited in Singapore's Carousell grey market. Confirm the seller is **iShopChangi, Shopee Mall, Lazada Official Store, Amazon.sg, or any physical Nintendo authorized retailer** (Courts, Harvey Norman, Best Denki, Challenger). Avoid:

- "Brand new, never used" Switch 2 listings below S$550 from non-mall Carousell sellers — these are modified Switch OLEDs flashed with Switch 2 firmware, not real Switch 2 units. Nintendo's firmware check is region-locked to Asia-Pacific consoles, and modchips break within 30 days.
- "Singapore set" vs "Japan set" listings claiming the Japan set is cheaper — the price differential vanished after Q4 2025 because Nintendo normalized SG/JP retail. Both regions now run the same firmware and receive the same game releases.
- "Digital code" sold by individuals — Nintendo eShop codes are tied to the region and non-refundable. Buy directly from the eShop or from authorized retailers.

Yes, if you want to play first-party Nintendo exclusives (Mario Kart World, Zelda TotK 2, Metroid Prime 4) at 4K docked output and have been holding off since the Switch OLED in 2017. **Can you use Singapore eShop codes on a US Switch 2?**
No. Nintendo eShop codes are region-locked. A Singapore-purchased code will only redeem on a Singapore-region console (NSX-Asia-Pacific firmware). Buy the console and the eShop code from the same region.

**Does the Switch 2 work on Singapore 240V power?**
Yes. The Switch 2 ships with a USB-C PD adapter rated 100-240V, 50/60Hz. Singapore's Type G plug requires no converter. The dock draws 39W max.

**How long is the Switch 2 warranty in Singapore?**
