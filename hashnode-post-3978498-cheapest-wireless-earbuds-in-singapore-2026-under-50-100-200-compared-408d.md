## What Singapore buyers should check

1. **Warranty**: Singapore authorized resellers (Apple Store, Challenger, Harvey Norman, Best Denki, Sony Center) offer 1-year local warranty. Lazada/Shopee third-party sellers may offer grey-market units with no local warranty.
2. **Codec support**: For Android, LDAC (Sony) and aptX (Sennheiser) deliver noticeably better sound than standard SBC. For iPhone, AAC is the only codec that matters.
3. **Battery + case**: 6+ hours per charge is the minimum acceptable. Wireless charging is a nice-to-have at S$100+.
4. **IP rating**: IPX4 minimum for gym use. IPX5+ if you run outdoors in Singapore rain.
5. **App support**: Sony, Soundcore, JBL, Sennheiser all have iOS/Android apps for EQ and firmware updates. Apple AirPods use system-level iOS/macOS settings.

## FAQ

**Are AirPods worth it over cheaper alternatives in Singapore?**
For iPhone users, yes — the integration (instant pairing, spatial audio, Find My) is unmatched. For Android users, Sony WF-C700N or Samsung Galaxy Buds FE offer better value.

**Where is the cheapest place to buy AirPods in Singapore?**
During non-sale periods, Challenger and Harvey Norman typically match Apple Store SRP. During 6.6, 7.7, 9.9, 11.11, 12.12 sales, Shopee Mall authorized sellers can drop 15–25%.

**Do Sony and Bose earbuds work with iPhone?**
Yes — both support standard Bluetooth and AAC codec. You lose LDAC (Sony) but otherwise functionality is full.

**Is it safe to buy earbuds from Lazada/Shopee third-party sellers?**
Check the seller rating (4.5+ stars, 95%+ positive) and confirm 1-year local warranty. Avoid sellers shipping from overseas — those won't have SG warranty.
