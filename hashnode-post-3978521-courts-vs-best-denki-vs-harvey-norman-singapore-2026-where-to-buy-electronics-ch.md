## Where each retailer wins

### Harvey Norman — TV and large appliance leader

Harvey Norman's [TV catalogue](https://www.harveynorman.com.sg/televisions) covers Samsung, LG, Sony, TCL, Hisense, and Panasonic with the widest selection in Singapore. They consistently match Best Denki on TVs and frequently beat them on flagship models by 3–5% during non-sale periods.

**Why Harvey Norman wins TVs:**
- Wider selection of OLED and QLED models
- Bundle deals (TV + soundbar) common
- Free delivery on orders above S$300
- Better trade-in program for old TVs

### Best Denki — Laptop and audio specialist

Best Denki's [laptop range](https://www.bestdenki.com.sg/computing/laptops) is the deepest in Singapore, particularly for Apple MacBooks, Microsoft Surface, and gaming laptops (ASUS ROG, Razer, Lenovo Legion). They run frequent member-only sales that undercut Harvey Norman by 5–10%.

**Why Best Denki wins laptops:**
- Authorized Apple Premium Reseller — full MacBook range
- Gaming laptop focus (Razer, ASUS ROG, MSI)
- Price match guarantee against Harvey Norman
- Free Microsoft Office bundle with Surface purchases (typical)

### Courts — Air conditioning and kitchen appliance king

Courts is the largest [air-conditioner retailer](https://www.courts.com.sg/air-conditioners) in Singapore by volume. Their installation service is industry-leading: free basic installation, same-day or next-day installation in most cases, and a 1-year workmanship warranty on top of the manufacturer's warranty.

**Why Courts wins air conditioning:**
- Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, Panasonic, LG, Samsung full range
- Free basic installation (worth S$200–S$400)
- 0% installment plans (up to 24 months via DBS/POSB/OCBC)
- Free removal of old unit
- Same-day installation available

## Sale calendar

All three retailers run quarterly mega-sales. The biggest price drops typically happen:

- **Great Singapore Sale (GSS):** June–July
- **9.9 Sale:** September (online-heavy)
- **11.11 Sale:** November (online-heavy)
- **12.12 Sale:** December (online-heavy)
- **Chinese New Year:** January–February
- **National Day:** August

During GSS, expect 15–25% off SRP at all three retailers, with Courts typically offering the deepest air conditioner discounts and Harvey Norman the deepest TV discounts.

## Warranty and after-sales comparison

All three retailers honor the full manufacturer's warranty (typically 1–2 years for electronics, 5–10 years for air conditioner compressors). Differences:

- **Courts**: 30-day no-questions-asked return on most items; extended warranty available (Courts Peace of Mind)
- **Best Denki**: 14-day return on unopened items; extended warranty available
- **Harvey Norman**: 30-day return; extended warranty available; free in-home setup for large appliances

## FAQ

**Does Best Denki price match Harvey Norman?**
Yes — Best Denki offers a 7-day price match against Harvey Norman and Courts for identical in-stock items. Bring proof (screenshot or printout) and they'll match.

**Is Courts cheaper than Harvey Norman for air conditioners?**
Typically yes by S$50–S$150, plus
 free installation that Harvey Norman charges for separately.

**Should I buy online or in-store?**
Online prices match in-store at all three retailers. The advantage of in-store is hands-on demo, immediate pickup, and easier returns. Online gives you Lazada/Shopee coupon stacking on top.

**Does Harvey Norman deliver for free?**
Yes, free delivery on orders above S$300. Below S$300, delivery is S$30–S$50 depending on item size.

**Which retailer has the best air conditioner installation?**
Courts, by a wide margin. Their installation team handles the most AC installations in Singapore annually.
