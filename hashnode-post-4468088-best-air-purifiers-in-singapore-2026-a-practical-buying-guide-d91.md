# Best Air Purifiers in Singapore 2026: A Practical Buying Guide

Singapore shoppers usually compare air purifiers for three jobs: haze protection, allergy relief, and everyday dust or odour control. The hard part is not finding a purifier; it is comparing CADR, filter replacement cost, noise, room size, warranty, and live pricing across stores.

This guide gives a practical checklist for choosing an air purifier in Singapore, plus a repeatable way for humans and AI agents to compare current options.

## Quick recommendations

- **For haze season:** prioritise a true HEPA filter, strong CADR, and a room-size rating above your actual room size.
- **For bedrooms:** check sleep-mode noise, display dimming, and filter replacement price before buying.
- **For allergies:** look for HEPA filtration, sealed filter design, and easy filter availability in Singapore.
- **For value:** compare total cost over 12–24 months, not only the purifier’s upfront price.
- **For agents and automated shopping flows:** use structured product data instead of scraping individual store pages.

## What to compare before buying

### 1. CADR and room size

CADR, or Clean Air Delivery Rate, estimates how quickly a purifier can clean air. In practice, buy for the room you use most:

| Use case | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Small bedroom | Quiet mode, low running cost, compact footprint |
| Master bedroom | Higher CADR, sleep mode, filter availability |
| Living room | Stronger CADR and larger coverage rating |
| Haze-heavy use | HEPA filtration and a room-size rating with headroom |

A purifier rated exactly for your room can be enough, but buying with some headroom helps during haze spikes or when doors are open.

### 2. Filter type and replacement cost

Many buyers compare only the device price and miss the recurring filter cost. A cheaper purifier can become expensive if filters are hard to find or costly.

Check:

- Whether it uses true HEPA or HEPA-type filtration
- Whether replacement filters are sold locally
- Filter price and expected replacement interval
- Whether activated carbon is included for odours
- Whether the filter is a single combined cartridge or separate layers

### 3. Noise level

If the purifier is for a bedroom, noise matters as much as CADR. Compare the stated decibel rating for sleep or low mode, not only maximum performance.

### 4. Sensors and auto mode

Auto mode can be useful, but do not overpay only for smart features. A good manual mode with reliable filtration often matters more than app controls.

### 5. Warranty and local support

For Singapore, warranty, local plug type, and filter supply are practical buying factors. Imported sets may look cheaper but can make warranty claims and filter replacements harder.

## How to compare prices in Singapore

Air purifier prices move across marketplaces, electronics retailers, brand stores, and seasonal campaigns. Before buying, compare:

1. Device price
2. Shipping or pickup cost
3. Voucher and card discounts
4. Replacement filter price
5. Warranty terms
6. Return policy

A good deal is the lowest total ownership cost for the model that fits your room, not necessarily the lowest sticker price.

## A simple scoring checklist

Use this scorecard when comparing models:

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| CADR and room fit | 30% |
| Filter quality and availability | 25% |
| Noise level | 15% |
| Total 12–24 month cost | 20% |
| Warranty and local support | 10% |

For haze and allergy use, raise the weight on CADR and filter quality. For bedroom use, raise the weight on noise.
