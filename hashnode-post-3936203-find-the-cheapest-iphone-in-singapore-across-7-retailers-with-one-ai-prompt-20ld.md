Want the best iPhone 16 Pro price in Singapore right now? FairPrice might have it at SGD 1,689 while Lazada lists it for SGD 1,749 — and that gap changes daily.

**Stop tab-hopping. Use an AI agent.**

## One prompt does it all

With BuyWhere MCP connected to your Claude Desktop:

> "Find the cheapest iPhone 16 Pro 256GB in Singapore. Show me all prices from FairPrice, Lazada, Shopee, Courts, Best Denki, Challenger, and Harvey Norman."

You get back live prices from every major Singapore retailer — not scraped yesterday, but right now.

## Why live data matters

Most shopping bots or scrapers show cached prices. BuyWhere hits each merchant's actual API/feed in real time. When Lazada runs a flash sale at 2 PM, your agent sees it at 2:01 PM.
