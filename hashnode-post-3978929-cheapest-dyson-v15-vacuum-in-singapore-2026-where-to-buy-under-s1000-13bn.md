## Is It Worth Buying the V15 Detect vs. the Cheaper V12 or V8?

- You have a large home (1,200+ sq ft) and need 60-min runtime

Skip it if:

- You have a small apartment (under 800 sq ft) — the V12 Detect Slim is S$500 cheaper and 90% as capable
