# HEPA vs Ionic Air Purifiers in Singapore: How to Compare Prices Without Guessing

If you're shopping for an air purifier in Singapore, the comparison usually starts with a simple question: **HEPA or ionic?**

The hard part is that the product pages rarely make this easy. Some listings lead with filter grade. Others lead with room size, CADR, noise level, or "ionizer" branding. Prices also move quickly across marketplaces, so a model that looked like the best deal yesterday may not be the best deal today.

This is where a structured product search layer helps. Instead of manually opening ten tabs, you can ask a BuyWhere MCP-connected assistant to compare real listings by the attributes that matter.

## Quick rule of thumb

For most homes, start with **true HEPA** if your priority is removing fine particles such as dust, haze, pollen, and pet dander.

Ionic purifiers can be useful in some contexts, but they are harder to compare because performance depends heavily on implementation, room conditions, and whether the model produces ozone. If a product page does not clearly disclose certification and safety details, treat that as a reason to slow down.

## What to compare before buying

When comparing air purifier prices in Singapore, look beyond the sticker price:

1. **Filter type** — true HEPA, HEPA-like, carbon filter, ionizer, UV, or hybrid.
2. **Room coverage** — match the advertised square metre rating to the room you actually use.
3. **CADR** — higher clean-air delivery rate usually means faster filtration.
4. **Replacement filter cost** — a cheap purifier can become expensive if filters are costly.
5. **Availability** — check whether replacement filters are easy to buy locally.
6. **Noise level** — especially for bedrooms and home offices.
7. **Warranty and merchant reliability** — not all listings are equal.
